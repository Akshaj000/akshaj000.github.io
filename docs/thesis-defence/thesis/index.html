Multimodal Affect Recognition for Unseen Visitors in a Free-Roaming VR Museum

Akshaj Somanpura Rajendran · Supervisor: Shenghui Wang

Can worn sensors tell a museum guide how a visitor feels, second by second, for someone it has never met? Across five experiments on a free-roaming VR visit, no sensor, combination or adaptation rose above chance, and the ratings show why: the painting explains almost none of a rating, the visitor most of it.
